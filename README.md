# Jovial Moons

Elegant single-page site for Jovial Moons.

Live: https://haizlcloud.github.io/jovialmoons_com/
## The hero: Moonrise

The hero is drawn in layers over `hero.jpg`'s own pixels (kept as the original
artwork): `sky.jpg` (the starfield, with the moons and Jupiter painted out),
the moons (drawn by the page's script, orbiting Jupiter — Io, Europa and
Ganymede 1 : 2 : 4, Ganymede in bronze, Io passing behind the planet) and
`jupiter.webp` (the planet and its glow, cut out to sit over them). Jupiter
rises with a dawn on its rim once per visit; transforms and opacity only, and
still under reduced motion.
